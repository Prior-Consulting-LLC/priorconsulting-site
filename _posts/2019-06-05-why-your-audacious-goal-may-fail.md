It's been a busy winter with lots of projects, clients and new writing goals. Topic: audacious goals and how they can fail. Years ago, Porras and Collins introduced BHAGs in "Built to Last." The main problem: setting the big vision is the easy part; it's how to get there that's the challenge.

Three tips:

1. **Monitor your internal dialogue** — self-doubt can trip you up
2. **Go for an experiment instead of perfection** — break off a component, try something small
3. **Reach out to someone who can help** — find a coach or mentor

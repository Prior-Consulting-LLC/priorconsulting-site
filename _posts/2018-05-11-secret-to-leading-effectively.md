The secret: Depth of Connection — the human glue that brings effective teams together.

Create Depth of Connection through Purpose, Relationships, and Development.

Lisa's 4 Easy Questions (4-EQ's):

1. Which role do I tend to play on teams?
2. How do I prefer to contribute and be included?
3. What is something I would like to learn or experience?
4. What do I need from this team to be successful?

Fall activity boils back in a rush. Author Meg Wheatley says you need an "island of sanity." Peer coaching can serve as that refuge.

Step 1: How to introduce peer coaching:

- Choose a format (1-1 or groups)
- Create a strong foundation (personal sharing, norms/agreements, commitments)
- Provide tools for self-awareness

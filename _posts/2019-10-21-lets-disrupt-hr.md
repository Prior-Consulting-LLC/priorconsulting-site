In the movie "Late Night," Mindy Kaling plays a career-shifter who leaps from project manager to comedy writer. The morning of her first day, she dresses corporate-style. Her younger sister asks: "Why are you dressed like you work in HR?" — a message that the HR profession is slow in adapting.

During my career as a leadership coach and culture change agent, I've helped leaders and companies disrupt so they can grow. Thank you, DisruptHR Boston, for the opportunity to speak November 6th. Through my talk, "WhoDunit? How Hollywood is Killing HR," I'll share views on what needs disrupting and the new roles HR can play.

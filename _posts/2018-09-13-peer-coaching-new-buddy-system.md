date: 2018-09-13
---
Scuba certification story introduces the buddy system concept. Peer coaching has surfaced as a crucial alternative to old mentoring programs.

Key differences from mentoring: mutual goal-setting, proven method, accountability, surfacing assumptions, feedback and feedforward.

Three steps to implement:

1. Create a strong foundation
2. Create success through method, skills and self-awareness
3. Make peer coaching a habit

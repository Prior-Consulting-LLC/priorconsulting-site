Organizational Design

We live in a world of Volatility, Uncertainty, Complexity, and Ambiguity. Some experts call this the VUCA Environment. The core job of today's leaders is twofold: to help their organizations move through predictable life cycles – from a startup entrepreneurial concern to one that's mature and decentralized – while also managing today's chaotic and rapidly changing world.

Leaders tackle these twin challenges successfully when they provide their people a different kind of “VUCA”: Vision, Understanding, Clarity, and Agility. These core capabilities are essential leadership skills as well as tools for organizational design. Vision, Understanding, Clarity, and Agility are the antidote to the traditional conception of VUCA.

Whether your organization is in the midst of unprecedented change or ready to move to the next stage in the organizational life cycle, Prior Consulting can help you create a new organizational design framework. Our goal is to help your company achieve its mission, meet new challenges, and sustain its competitive advantages.

Executive Talent Strategy

As two interconnected processes, Talent Reviews and Succession Planning help businesses identify employees with high potential across the organization. Once talent has been identified, organizations can then implement a process to successfully develop these employees while identifying specific individuals for future key positions.

Whether your company is young and growing or mature and changing, Prior Consulting designs custom approaches for developing talent for today and tomorrow.
